ABOUT KOGAME

Giving great games a new home.

We’re an independent seller and collector based in Ireland, with a passion for retro gaming and pop culture. We enjoy finding, testing and giving pre-owned games, consoles, accessories and collectibles a new life.

With a particular love for the 90s and early 2000s, every item is carefully checked and honestly described before being listed. Whether you’re a collector, a casual player or simply rediscovering an old favourite, KOGAME is here to make buying pre-owned gaming gear feel straightforward and trustworthy.
